Privacy policy

Last updated October 3, 2026

This policy covers the personal assistant app that Mike Edmonds runs on his own home server. The app is for his personal use only and is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With permission granted through Google sign-in, the app can read the owner's Gmail messages and calendar, and create email drafts for him to review. It does not send or delete email on its own.

How the data is used

Data from Google is used only to help the owner manage his own email and schedule. It stays on his self-hosted server and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page, which also stops all further data access by the app.
