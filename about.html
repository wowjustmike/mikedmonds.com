Log About Work with me

Curious by nature, technician by trade.

I didn't start in IT. I started in marketing, picked up a camera and got serious about photography, then spent years in retail management keeping stores, staff and customers running.

In 2026 I turned the thing I'd always done for fun — taking computers apart and figuring out why they broke — into the job. Today I'm a field and bench technician at EMS Mobile Computer Service in Las Vegas.

I'm still early in this career, and I like it that way — there's something new to learn every week. What carries over from every job I've had is the same habit: figure out how something works, help it work better, and explain it clearly.
